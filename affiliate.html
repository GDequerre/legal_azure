Program details

Commission

30% of every payment the referred customer makes to Azure Flowstate for the Hirry service or the Hirry AI service.

Cookie window

60 days. If the customer signs up within 60 days of clicking your link, the sale is attributed to you.

Payout cadence

Monthly, by bank transfer (SEPA / SWIFT) once you reach the minimum payout threshold (EUR 100 or local equivalent).

Refunds & clawbacks

If a customer payment is refunded (including under the delivery guarantee in the Hirry Terms), the matching commission is reversed.

FAQ

Short answers to what people usually ask.

Who can apply?

Anyone with an audience or channel that includes small and medium business owners in Europe. Content creators, recruiters, consultants, agencies, communities, newsletters — all welcome to apply.

How long until I am accepted?

We review applications within five working days and email a yes/no with a short reason.

Is the 30% on the gross amount the customer pays, or net of taxes?

30% of the amount actually collected by Azure Flowstate, net of taxes and payment processing fees that we pass through.

Can I get paid for multiple Hirry AI seats from the same customer?

Yes. Additional agentic AI seats are billed at the same rate and cadence as the original Hirry AI subscription, and you earn 30% on each of those payments too, for the duration of their 36-month term.

What happens if the customer doesn't pay?

You earn on payments collected, not on contracts signed. If a customer stops paying, your commission stream stops with theirs.

Can I be both a customer and an affiliate?

You can, but you cannot earn commission on your own subscriptions.

What if I bring an existing Azure Flowstate customer?

Referrals must be new customers. We deduplicate against our existing customer base before attributing a sale.
