## 4. The delivery guarantee

Hirry markets itself with a delivery guarantee. The specific commitment that applies to your subscription is the **variant** assigned to your account at sign-up and shown in your platform dashboard:

- **Variant A**: 10 pre-qualified candidates delivered within 48 hours, or the entry fee is refunded.
- **Variant B**: 2 interviews held within 7 days with candidates matching all stated criteria, or the entry fee is refunded.
- **Variant C**: 1 hire within 30 days, or the entry fee is refunded.

For the guarantee to apply:

1. You must have completed your search configuration on the platform within 24 hours of saving your card;
2. The criteria you set must be lawful, non-discriminatory, and realistic for the local labour market;
3. "Pre-qualified candidate" means a person who has been contacted by Hirry, has confirmed availability, and matches the criteria you defined. Candidates who do not match your stated criteria do not count toward the guarantee;
4. "Interview held" means a scheduled meeting (in person, by phone, or by video) that actually took place between you and the candidate;
5. "Hire" means an employment offer extended by you and accepted by the candidate.

If the guarantee triggers in your favour, the refund is processed to the original payment method within 30 days of your written request.
