Deletion of your Data

Last updated: 28 May 2026

Under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), you have the right to request the deletion of personal data we hold about you, collected through our "Verification Assistance" service on WhatsApp.

How to request deletion of your data

  1. By email: send your request to formalcomplaints@azure.msg.community with the subject line "WhatsApp Data Deletion Request".
  2. Information to provide: include the phone number associated with the WhatsApp account you used to contact us.
  3. Verification: we may ask you to confirm your identity before processing your request.
  4. Processing time: your request will be processed within a maximum of 30 days.

Data covered

Deletion will cover:

  • Your phone number
  • The history of your conversations with our service
  • Any personal information you provided to us via WhatsApp
Exceptions: Certain data may be retained beyond your deletion request where required by law, in particular:
  • Records required for accounting and tax obligations under Singapore law
  • Records required for anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing obligations, where applicable
  • Data necessary for the management of an active dispute or legal claim

Automatic deletion

In the absence of a request from you, your data is automatically deleted one (1) year after your last interaction with our service, subject to the legal exceptions described above.

Confirmation

Once your request has been processed, we will send a confirmation to the email address you provide.
