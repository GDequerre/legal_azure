Deletion of your Data

Last updated: 28 May 2026

Under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), and where applicable the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request the deletion of personal data we hold about you, collected through our AI-driven recruitment services (azure.msg.community, hirry.co, and WhatsApp Business).

Who can request deletion

  • Candidates — individuals contacted by our agents or who interacted with our platform.
  • Customers and their representatives — employer accounts (subject to legal record-keeping obligations, see exceptions below).

How to request deletion of your data

  1. By email: send your request to formalcomplaints@azure.msg.community with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Information to provide: include the phone number, WhatsApp number, or email address associated with your interaction with us. For Customer accounts, include the company name and account holder's name.
  3. Verification: we may ask you to confirm your identity before processing your request.
  4. Processing time: your request will be processed within a maximum of 30 days.

Data covered

Deletion will cover:

  • Your contact details (name, phone, WhatsApp, email);
  • Resume content and any other documents you submitted (for Candidates);
  • Job descriptions and hiring criteria you submitted (for Customers, subject to dispute-management retention);
  • The history of your conversations with our agents (WhatsApp, website chat, voice call recordings and transcripts);
  • AI-generated content derived from your inputs.
Exceptions: Certain data may be retained beyond your deletion request where required by law or for the protection of legal rights, in particular:
  • Records required for accounting and tax obligations under Singapore law (typically 5 years);
  • Records required for anti-money-laundering or counter-terrorism-financing obligations, where applicable;
  • Data necessary for the management of an active dispute or legal claim;
  • Aggregated or anonymised data that no longer identifies you, which may be retained indefinitely for product improvement.

Automatic deletion

In the absence of a request from you, your data is automatically deleted in line with the retention schedule set out in our Privacy Policy — in summary:

  • Candidate data: 1 year from your last interaction.
  • Message and call recordings: 12 months from the interaction.
  • Customer account data: kept while the account is active; 6 years after termination for accounting and dispute purposes.

Confirmation

Once your request has been processed, we will send a confirmation to the email address you provide.
